## Featured Projects

### Korean Doctors · AI consult assistant for visitors to Korea

<img src="./assets/korean-doctors-app.jpg" alt="Korean Doctors app screens: a conversation with the AI assistant Kim, the photo step, and procedure information" width="820" />

A mobile app that helps international visitors see which aesthetic procedures exist for their concerns before they travel.

- **Kim**, an AI consult assistant, leads a free-form conversation in English, Arabic or Korean
- A photo analysis step adds context, and a rule engine organises the relevant procedure information
- Kim informs and never decides: a doctor makes the final judgment in person
- Photos are analysed without being stored on a server and go only to the clinic the user picks

**Role:** product concept, UX and brand design, AI agent design · **Status:** in development, 2027

### Jeju Racing · real-time English form guide

A site that collects race data from Korea Racing Authority public data and uses AI to generate English commentary and form guides for Jeju horse racing. Planned and built end to end, from data sourcing to the prototype and vendor procurement.

[Live prototype](https://krainternational.github.io/jeju-racecard/)

### Ryoony · AI interview agent

An LLM agent that answers interview questions only from my own career records, shows the source cards behind each answer, and forwards anything it cannot answer to me by email. Korean and English.

[Try it on the portfolio](https://jokim90.github.io/#ryoony)
